## 📝 Full / Long Description

**Drawora — The modern, effortless sketch canvas built to capture ideas at the speed of thought.**

Whether you’re jotting down a sudden flash of inspiration, creating expressive digital sketches, illustrating concept wireframes, or simply doodling to unwind, **Drawora** delivers a seamless, zero-latency drawing experience wrapped in a sleek, distraction-free interface.

Designed for creators, students, designers, and visual thinkers, Drawora combines responsive touch precision with essential pro-grade tools to give your imagination complete freedom.

---

### ✨ Key Features

🎨 **Fluid, Zero-Latency Canvas**
Experience lightning-fast stroke responsiveness. Drawora’s high-performance drawing engine tracks every movement with natural precision, delivering smooth lines and zero lag.

🌈 **Curated Vibrance & Precision Tools**
* **Tailored Color Palette**: Pick from a hand-picked, dual-mode color palette crafted to look stunning on both light and dark backgrounds.
* **Live Brush Sizing**: Adjust stroke thickness from delicate 2px pencil lines to bold 32px marker strokes with real-time visual thickness feedback.
* **Smart Eraser**: Clean up lines effortlessly with natural background revelation.

🌓 **Dynamic Light & Dark Themes**
Switch seamlessly between an energizing crisp white canvas for daylight sketching and a deep slate dark mode for comfortable late-night creativity. The canvas, tools, and UI adapt automatically to maintain optimal contrast and reduced eye strain.

🔄 **Unlimited Undo & Redo**
Draw with zero fear of mistakes. Jump backward and forward through your stroke history with our connected quick-action pill capsule. Erase all in one tap when you’re ready for a fresh blank canvas.

📤 **1-Tap High-Resolution Export**
Save and share your sketches in crystal-clear, full HD PNG format. Export directly to messaging apps, social platforms, cloud drives, or device storage with a single tap.

🛡️ **Privacy-First & 100% Offline**
Your artwork belongs exclusively to you. Drawora requires no sign-in, no accounts, and works completely offline without uploading your drawings to external servers.
